# QuestionnaireResponse is one record with a status, not separate Draft/Submission entities

We need to support autosaving partial questionnaire answers (so a user who exits early keeps their progress and stays gated behind the login redirect until they actually submit) without losing the existing "does a response exist for this identity" completion check. We considered modeling in-progress answers as a separate Draft entity that gets promoted to a Submission on completion, but chose instead to keep a single `QuestionnaireResponse` per identity (`user_id`/`device_id`) with a `status` of `in_progress` or `submitted`: answers accumulate in place via autosave, and submitting just flips the status. This avoids a migration/promotion step and keeps one row per identity, at the cost of the completion check changing from "row exists" to "status == submitted."
